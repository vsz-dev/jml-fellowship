September 2026

Walking in the truth that sets free

John 8:31–36

This month we are studying what it means to know the truth and let it hold us steady — in our thoughts, our homes and our decisions. Jesus tells His disciples that continuing in His word is what makes them truly His, and that the truth they come to know is what sets them free.

Each week we are working through one part of this passage in our small groups: what it means to continue in the word, what freedom actually looks like day to day, and how a fellowship becomes a family that carries each other toward it.

Come with your Bible and a notebook. Bring one question you're sitting with — that is usually where the best study happens.
